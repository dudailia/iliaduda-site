## Design Specificity Verdict
**LLM assessment.** Authored, not interchangeable, and it survives the phone. The working-paper register (a running head
in mono, "Fig. N" with a mono subtitle that says "Simulated … not market data", margin labels folded inline, indigo only
on the claimed value) is the site's own, and the live figures are real computations (a million-path Monte Carlo priced
against Black–Scholes on the reader's GPU, a Hawkes order book, an SSVI surface) that a neighbouring portfolio could copy
in type but not in substance. On a phone the stages run edge to edge, which gives them presence without chrome. The only
generic parts are the small bordered buttons, and they are quiet. Missed opportunity: an emailed paper link opens on a
text-only first screen at every phone size; the figure that is the paper's argument starts one scroll down (0–121 px of
the stage at 390×844, none in Safari's 659 px).

**Deterministic scan.** `impeccable detect --json app components` → [] (exit 0); also [] with `--no-config` and
`--no-inline-ignores`. A scratch copy without the config surfaces only the three configured ignores, all false
positives: two next/font family aliases (app/globals.css:341, :349) and the ?debug overlay's 11 px
(components/stage/Debug.tsx:81). URL-mode scans (12 routes × 3 sizes): 69 warnings in 15 of 36 — line-length 38 (the CV
sheet at Letter scale, mono subtitle and hint lines, the scanner's width estimate; the prose measure is 44.375rem by
design), clipped-overflow-container 6 (stages clip by design), buried-raster 6 (posters under live canvases),
cramped-padding 10 and tight-leading 6 (the paper tags on single-line stage labels), layout-transition 3 on /order-book
(no height transition in app/ or components/; unconfirmed). None is a true issue; the detector caught nothing the review
missed, and every material finding below is behavioural (start gates, landscape geometry, WebKit form controls, input
feedback), which a static or DOM scan cannot see.

**Visual overlays.** Not injected, so no user-visible overlay: the owner's constraint forbids starting any server
(including `impeccable live-server`), and the page's CSP (`script-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'`) refuses a script from
http://localhost:8400 anyway (preflight: an inline script runs, the external detect.js is blocked). Fallback signal:
the URL-mode scans above.

## Overall Impression
On a phone this is the best the site has been. Each paper's one moment is a real event (the home burst → histogram →
price → swing into depth; the order book rising out of a grey page into indigo walls; /market's shock landing in three
views in one frame), each leans with tilt and glows at night, and around them the pages stay quiet. WebKit (iPhone)
draws the same pictures at the same quality, 56–61 frames a second. What holds it down is not the look but timing and
geometry at the edges: one signature plays unseen on tall phones, one stage cannot fit a phone turned sideways, one form
is silent when it is wrong. The single biggest opportunity: make "one signature moment per page" hold on every phone by
gating each story on what the reader can actually see.

## What's Working
- **The live figures survive the phone intact.** A thumb scroll 18 px off vertical over any stage scrolls the page 245
  of 260 px and leaves the figure alone; a sideways drag turns it without scrolling; tilt leans it ("lean from tilt 0.77,
  −0.73"); Pause stops the pixels within 250–500 ms; Fly through comes home at a touch; every one plays in view on
  390×844 and 360×780 (except the IV surface) and completes.
- **The hiring essentials are never more than one screen away.** On /, name, availability, CV, email, LinkedIn and
  GitHub end above the fold at 390×844, 360×780 and 844×390 (the contact row ends at 633, 633 and 362 px); every paper's
  running head carries name, "Co-op Jan 2027", CV and Email.
- **The Contents is a table of contents, not a carousel.** Miniatures load only when half on screen, draw at the box's
  own pixels (1020×610 for a 342×205 box), and only one moves at a time (the market's, then the cricket's as it nears
  the middle; the rest pixel-identical); Pause holds all and relabels "Resume the thumbnails"; a tap morphs the
  thumbnail into the paper, which lands with its figure 56 px from the top; back restores the scroll. Same in WebKit and
  in dark.

## Priority Issues
**[P1] /iv-surface tells its story below the fold on tall phones.**
- *What:* opened from a link with nothing scrolled, 78 of the stage's 355 px show at 390×844 (22%) and 101 of 375 on a
  Pixel 7 (412×839, 27%); the default rule (a fifth held 1.2 s) starts the 5 s story anyway — debug "pending" at 1.5 s,
  "done" at 7 s, scrollY 0.
- *Why:* the page's one orchestrated moment forms and takes its shock under the fold; the reader who scrolls finds it
  over. The order book was given `{ start: 0.6, hold: 0.45 }` for exactly this; iPhone 15 in Safari (659 px) and 15 Pro
  Max (739 px) happen to wait.
- *Location:* `components/figures/surface/Live.tsx:300` (`useSignature('surface', box, seq)`), defaults at
  `components/stage/useSignature.ts:24`; compare `components/figures/orderbook/Live.tsx:238`.
- *Fix:* `useSignature('surface', box, seq, { start: 0.6, hold: 0.45 })`, and an e2e at 390×844 and 412×839 expecting
  "pending" after 7 s unscrolled and "done" once scrolled in.
- *Command:* $impeccable harden

**[P2] /market's three views never fit a phone turned sideways, and the shock can land with the surface off screen.**
- *What:* at 844×390 the stage is 617–684 px tall in 390; the story's gate is the lower row alone. Scrolled until the
  futures' axis shows, it goes "pending" → "playing" with the surface's top 267 px above the screen (77 of 343 visible).
- *Why:* the page's claim is that the shock "lands in all three at once"; in landscape the reader sees two.
- *Location:* `components/figures/market/Live.tsx:370` (gate) and `:978–1036` (surface above `lowerRow`, lowerRow grid
  at `:1036`).
- *Fix:* under `[@media(max-height:30rem)]` (the masthead's query), lay the stage out two-up — surface left (capped by
  the existing 88svh), book over futures right — so it fits 390 px; gate the story on the whole stage.
- *Command:* $impeccable adapt

**[P2] Sliders lose their indigo on WebKit (iPhone parity).**
- *What:* in the iPhone 15 profile every range input draws the platform's grey track with a white, shadowed knob;
  Chromium draws the indigo track and thumb. DESIGN.md names "a slider's filled track" as a Measurement Indigo use, and
  the no-shadow rule is broken by the native knob. Evidence: A/shots/live/wk-390x844-light-home-paused2.png,
  B/sheets/parity-390l.png.
- *Location:* the only styling is `accent-[var(--color-indigo)]`/`accent-indigo` at
  `components/figures/futures/Live.tsx:937`, `:955`; `surface/Live.tsx:706`; `surface/Bound.tsx:124`;
  `settlement/Live.tsx:183`; `ofz/Live.tsx:209`; `cricket/Live.tsx:352`.
- *Fix:* one shared range style in `app/globals.css`: `appearance:none`; `::-webkit-slider-runnable-track` and
  `::-moz-range-track` a 2 px track filled `linear-gradient(var(--color-indigo) 0 var(--fill), var(--color-rule) 0)`; a
  16 px indigo thumb with a 2 px paper ring, no shadow; each figure sets `--fill` from its value. Confirm on the device
  (iOS Safari's accent-color on range may differ from this build).
- *Command:* $impeccable polish

**[P2] /debt-portal answers a wrong amount only to screen readers.**
- *What:* typing 50 in "Can pay per month, ₽" silently selects 36 months at 1,584 ₽ a month (32× what the reader said

- *Command:* $impeccable clarify

## Questions to Consider
- Should a paper opened from an email show a glimpse of its live figure on a phone's first screen — a line under the
  byline in the figure's own ink ("Fig. 1, live, below"), or a tighter phone title size — so the "alive" promise does not
  wait a scroll?
- Should every signature be gated on what the reader can see of its story rather than on a share of its box? The order
  book and /market moved to that; the IV surface is the one left.
- Is landscape a real reading posture for this audience? If yes, /market is the one page to redesign for it; if not,
  should the page say its story plays upright?
