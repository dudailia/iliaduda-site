## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** Authored, not category-interchangeable. The journal-issue structure (masthead front matter, Fig. N in
a rail, hint line, graphite caption, sources block with file paths, every page ending on the ask) holds on all twelve
routes in both themes, and the figures are the argument: Fig. 1 converges live on Black–Scholes with its gap in
standard errors in the rail; the IV rail reads "No static arbitrage passes, min g 0.268, on 2,440 grid points". Where
it slips toward generic: the masthead's second sentence ("I work on quantitative finance and the systems around it"),
disc bullet lists on /about, /market and /order-book, and the two WebGL surfaces, which read as extruded CAD slabs at
night beside the glowing futures and order book.

**Deterministic scan.** `impeccable detect --json app components`: 0 findings (122 files; also 0 with --no-config and
--no-inline-ignores). The project's three ignoreValues hide exactly three false positives (the font supplement's
family aliases at app/globals.css:341 and :349; the ?debug=1 overlay's 11 px at components/stage/Debug.tsx:81). URL scans
at 1440×900 and 1280×800: 43 warnings each, none a new real defect — line-length ×29 (21 on the /cv sheet, by design;
the rest the site-wide caption measure), first-viewport-column-overflow ×5–6 (the sticky-rail grid), clipped-overflow
×2 and buried-raster ×2 (intentional stage clips and the posters under live canvases), tight-leading ×2 (single-line
order-flow labels), cramped-padding ×2 (the IV narration plate, P3), layout-transition ×1 (/order-book, P3). The
detector could not see either P1 or four of the five P2s: they are composition, copy, timing and rendering.

**Browser evidence.** 72 loads: 0 axe WCAG A/AA violations, 0 console errors, 0 failed or third-party requests, 0 CSP
violations, 0 long tasks, max CLS 0.00357, one h1 and ordered headings everywhere, no overflow. All four live figures
draw nothing after Pause and stop off screen. The browser pass confirmed two of A's findings independently (the IV
narration overlap, 24 px at both sizes and both themes; /market's order book wholly below the fold at both sizes) and
added one A missed (∅ on /startup-investments drawn in the system mono).

**Visual overlays.** None. The overlay needs `impeccable live-server`, which the brief forbids starting; and the
site's CSP (`script-src 'self'`) blocks an external detect.js even if it were running (preflight: title set and an
inline script ran; the external script raised a securitypolicyviolation). The CLI's URL scans stood in.

## Overall Impression

The best version of this site yet: a coherent working paper whose three strongest figures now play their signatures
on a laptop's first screen and stay alive, calmly, with a Pause that really stops them, and a technical floor (a11y,
CLS, CSP, fonts, network) that is essentially clean across 72 loads. The single biggest opportunity is the lead paper:
/market's three views never fit a laptop screen, so the site's "most dramatic moment" starts only after an unprompted
scroll; and the first screen still hides the strongest credential, the current prop-trading role.

## What's Working

1. The figures prove the claims in front of the reader: live convergence against the formula with its error band,
   arbitrage checks named with their margin, Hawkes flow at ~300 events a second, all labelled synthetic where they are.
2. The paper system survives every route and both themes: rail, Fig. N, hint, caption, sources; indigo only on the
   claim; the 404 and every footer route back to the ask ("The fastest way to reach me is email.").
3. Motion discipline, measured: one signature a page; one Contents miniature at a time (only the entry nearest
   mid-screen changes pixels; hover hands the motion to the hovered entry); Pause leaves the stage pixel-identical;
   reduced motion gives complete, readable still frames.
